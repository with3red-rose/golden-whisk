GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I order?

Choose a cake from the collection (or ask for a custom design) and send the enquiry form. We reply to confirm availability, design and your final price. Your order is only confirmed once we have agreed these details with you.

How much notice do you need?

Please enquire at least 3–5 days ahead. Wedding and highly detailed designs need more notice, so contact us as early as you can.

How does payment work?

No payment is taken on the website. After we confirm your quote, we share payment details directly. A deposit may be required to secure your date; the balance is due before or on collection, as agreed.

Where do I collect?

Orders are collected in Johannesburg, Gauteng. We share the exact address and time once your order is confirmed.

Allergies and dietary needs

Please tell us about any allergies when you enquire. Our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts and soy, so we cannot guarantee that any product is free from traces of allergens.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if your plans change. See our terms for how changes and cancellations are handled.

Check on an existing order

Message us on WhatsApp or email us and quote your order reference.
