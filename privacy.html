YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

The Golden Whisk respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

The Golden Whisk, Johannesburg, Gauteng, is the responsible party for your personal information. Contact us at our email address or on WhatsApp.

What we collect

When you send an order enquiry we collect your name, phone number, optional email address, and the order details you give us (cake, date, notes and any allergies). Allergy or dietary details can relate to your health, so we use them only to prepare your order safely.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare and arrange collection of your order, and handle payment and queries about it. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is delivered to us by Formspree, our form provider, which passes it to our email inbox. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp (Meta). We share information with others only where needed to provide the service or where the law requires.

Storage outside South Africa

Formspree and WhatsApp are operated outside South Africa, so your information may be processed in other countries. By submitting the form you agree to this transfer for the purpose of handling your enquiry.

How long we keep it

We keep order enquiries for 12 months after your collection date, or longer where the law requires us to keep records, and then delete them. Enquiries are held in our email inbox and in our Formspree account.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its use, by contacting us at our email address. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies and third-party content

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and some photos from Unsplash, which may receive your IP address when you visit.
