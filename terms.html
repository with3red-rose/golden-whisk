Terms of sale.

Enquiries and orders

Submitting the website form is an enquiry, not a confirmed order. An order is confirmed only when we confirm the design, price and collection details with you.

Prices

Prices on the website are starting prices in South African rand. The final price depends on size, flavour, design and extras, and is confirmed in your quote.

Deposits and payment

We may require a deposit to secure your date. Payment details are shared directly by us. The balance is payable as agreed, no later than collection.

Changes and cancellations

Changes are accepted where possible, depending on how far ahead we are told. Because every cake is made to order, deposits may be non-refundable once ingredients are bought or baking has begun. We will always explain this when we confirm your order.

Collection and care

Please collect at the agreed time. Cakes are perishable; follow the storage advice we give you. Once collected, handling and transport are the customer's responsibility.

Allergens

Our products may contain or come into contact with gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts and soy. Please tell us about allergies before ordering.

Your consumer rights

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act. Questions? Email us.
